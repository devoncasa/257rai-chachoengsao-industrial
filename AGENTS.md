# Thai editorial conventions

- Write investor-facing Thai as clear, formal business discussion: explain the opportunity, its practical relevance, and the evidence needed to evaluate it.
- Use familiar Thai property, logistics, and investment terminology. Explain necessary technical terms when first introduced. Avoid literal English sentence structures, compressed slogans, and unexplained jargon.
- Attach ๆ directly to the preceding word: ต่างๆ, อื่นๆ. Never insert a space before ๆ. Apply this to Thai website text and project communications.
- Preserve verified facts, figures, source links and distinctions between regional opportunities and parcel-specific approvals. Rewriting must not silently remove substantive information.
